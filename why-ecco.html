Why Ecco

Why homeowners choose Ecco

Instant guide prices and trusted local installers, free to use. Here's what makes Ecco different.

Home upgrades, without the guesswork

Getting a boiler, rewire, solar or heat pump quote usually means phone calls, home visits, vague estimates and prices that creep up. Ecco was built to fix that: answer a few questions, get an instant guide price, and we'll match you with a trusted local installer — all from your phone.

💷 Honest guide prices

See a realistic guide price up front, including materials, labour, certification and VAT. Your installer confirms the final price before any work starts.

🛡️ Trusted local installers

We pass your request to local installers registered for the job — Gas Safe for gas, a registered electrician for electrics, MCS for renewables.

✅ Free, no obligation

Ecco is free for homeowners. You only go ahead if you're happy with the price your installer confirms.

📱 All from your phone

No phone calls or home visits just to get a price. Answer a few questions and we do the legwork.

How Ecco works

Ecco matches homeowners with trusted local installers. When you send a request, we share your answers with one or more installers in your area so they can confirm your price. That's it — no hard sell.

Built by people who do the work

Ecco is built by people with real installation experience in the North West — so the questions we ask and the guide prices we show are grounded in how the work actually gets done. We only share your details with installers to get you a quote, as set out in our privacy notice.
