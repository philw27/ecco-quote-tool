How Ecco works

From your first question to the final install and beyond, our team of guides makes upgrading your home simple, clear and stress-free — and every job is independently signed off before it's done.

Step 1

Tell us about your home

Answer a few quick questions about your property and what you need. No waiting around — just a simple set of questions you can do from your sofa. You'll get a guide price online; your installer will confirm with a quick survey.

Rico, your planning guide, uses your answers to find the right options.
Step 2

We find the right options

We use your answers to suggest options that suit your home and current UK regulations.

Atlas, our compliance guide, makes sure everything meets the standard.
Step 3

Get your guide price

You get an instant guide price on screen, including materials, labour, certification and VAT. Your installer confirms the final price before any work starts.

Dash gets your request to the right people in your area.
Step 4

A local installer gets in touch

We pass your request to a trusted local installer, who confirms your price and arranges a date that suits you. Ecco is free to use and there's no obligation.

Bruno makes sure your request reaches the right installer.
Step 5

The installer does the job

Your installer carries out the work, removes the waste and gives you your certificates. Your contract is with them, so check the quote before you go ahead.

Atlas reminds you to check your installer's registrations.
Step 6

We're here after, too

If anything isn't right, let us know and we'll help sort it with your installer.

Otis, your customer care guide, keeps you looked after.
