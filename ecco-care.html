How to join

1

Installed through Ecco? Joining is simpler

If your system was fitted by an installer we matched you with, tell us when you register — it may join with no survey. Just join within 30 days of your install.

2

Electrical cover needs a valid EICR

If we didn't wire it (or it's been more than 30 days), your electrics need a satisfactory EICR before joining — £220, which is also your electrical safety certificate. Pass it and you're on.

3

Gas, solar & heat pumps — a quick photo survey

For everything else, just upload a few photos of your system. We'll assess it remotely and let you know if it's good to go — no appointment needed.
